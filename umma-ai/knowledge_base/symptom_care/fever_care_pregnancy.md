# SYMPTOM CARE FILE: FEVER IN PREGNANCY
## Nigerian Context — Awaiting Clinician Verification

---

## ACTION TIERS

### EMERGENCY — Go to hospital immediately
Triggered by any of:
- Seizure or convulsion
- Confusion or loss of consciousness
- No fetal movement
- Severe difficulty breathing
- Heavy vaginal bleeding with fever
- Stiff neck
- Unable to stand or walk due to fever

Plain language: "This is very serious and needs immediate hospital care. Please go now — do not wait."

---

### URGENT CLINIC — Go today, same day
Triggered by any of:
- Any fever in pregnancy with no confirmed safe cause
- Suspected malaria — even mild
- Suspected kidney infection
- Suspected typhoid
- Fever lasting more than 24 hours
- Fever with vomiting unable to keep fluids down
- Any fever in third trimester
- Any uncertainty about cause

Plain language: "You need to be seen by a doctor or nurse today. Please do not wait until tomorrow."

---

### HOME CARE — Only if ALL of these are true
- Fever is very mild and just started
- No red flags present
- Baby moving normally
- No vomiting
- Able to drink fluids well
- Cause is clearly identified as non-serious

Home care instructions:
- Rest completely
- Drink plenty of clean water and fluids
- Paracetamol only for fever — do not take aspirin or ibuprofen in pregnancy
- Do not take any herbal remedies
- Monitor baby movements
- Check temperature regularly
- Return immediately if any red flag develops

Plain language: "You can rest at home for now but please watch carefully and go to the clinic today if you do not feel better."

---

### MONITOR — With very clear safety net
- Mild fever just started, baby moving, drinking well, no red flags
- Must re-evaluate within a few hours
- Any worsening — move to urgent clinic immediately

---

## SAFETY NET — Attach to EVERY response about fever in pregnancy
"Please watch carefully. Go to hospital immediately if your fever gets much worse, you start shaking and cannot stop, your vision becomes blurry, you have a fit, your baby stops moving, or you feel confused. Do not wait if you are worried — it is always better to be seen by a doctor."

---

## GENERAL SAFE ADVICE FOR FEVER IN PREGNANCY
- Paracetamol is the only safe medicine for fever in pregnancy — follow correct dose
- Do not take aspirin — dangerous in pregnancy
- Do not take ibuprofen — dangerous in pregnancy
- Do not take any herbal medicines or local remedies for fever in pregnancy
- Drink plenty of fluids — water, clean juice, oral rehydration solution
- Rest completely
- Sleep under a mosquito net every night
- Do not try to diagnose or treat malaria at home without a blood test
- Go to antenatal clinic as scheduled even if feeling better

---

## NIGERIAN SPECIFIC CARE ADVICE
- In hot northern Nigerian climate — ensure room is ventilated and cool
- Mosquito net use is essential — malaria prevention critical in pregnancy
- Do not use local herbal steam treatments for fever in pregnancy
- Encourage husband or family support for clinic visit — cultural barrier awareness
- Nearest primary health centre is appropriate first point of care if teaching hospital is far
