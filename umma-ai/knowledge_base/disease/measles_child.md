# DISEASE FILE: MEASLES IN A CHILD UNDER 5
## Nigerian Context (Kano / Northwest Priority) — Awaiting Clinician Verification

---

## OVERVIEW
Measles remains a significant cause of child morbidity and mortality in
northern Nigeria. Kano State has experienced recurrent measles outbreaks
due to persistently low vaccination coverage in some communities. Vaccine
hesitancy, geographical barriers, and inadequate routine immunisation
service delivery contribute to coverage gaps. Measles itself is rarely
fatal in well-nourished children — but in Nigeria's context, measles
kills primarily through its complications: pneumonia, diarrhoea, severe
malnutrition, and encephalitis. Malnourished children — who make up over
60% of Kano's under-5 population — are at dramatically higher risk of
fatal measles complications. Vitamin A deficiency, common in northern
Nigeria, greatly worsens measles severity and causes blindness.
Measles is highly contagious — a single case should prompt assessment
of other unvaccinated children in the household.

## HOW IT PRESENTS

### Prodromal Phase — Days 1 to 4 Before Rash
- High fever — often the first sign
- Runny nose
- Cough
- Red watery eyes — conjunctivitis
- Child looks unwell and miserable
- Koplik spots — tiny white spots inside the cheeks, opposite the
  lower molars — pathognomonic for measles, appear before rash

### Rash Phase — Days 4 to 7
- Rash appears first behind ears and on hairline
- Spreads to face, then neck, then trunk, then arms and legs — top to bottom
- Rash is red, blotchy, and may merge — maculopapular
- Fever often highest as rash appears
- Child looks most unwell during rash phase

### Recovery Phase — Days 7 to 10
- Rash begins to fade — darkens and peels from top to bottom
- Fever settles if no complications
- Child gradually improves

### Complicated Measles — High Risk in Kano
- Pneumonia — most common cause of measles death
- Diarrhoea — second most common cause of measles death
- Ear infection — otitis media
- Mouth ulcers — stomatitis — severe in malnourished children
- Corneal ulceration and blindness — from vitamin A deficiency
- Measles encephalitis — rare but devastating, seizures and coma
- Severe protein-energy malnutrition triggered by measles illness
- Reactivation of tuberculosis

---

## DIFFERENTIATING FEATURES
- Classic prodrome of fever + cough + runny nose + red eyes before rash
  is highly specific for measles
- Koplik spots inside mouth — confirm measles before rash appears
- Rash spreading head to toe — distinguishes from other rashes
- Unvaccinated or incompletely vaccinated child
- Outbreak in school, mosque, or community
- Rash in measles does NOT blister and does NOT have fluid inside —
  distinguishes from chickenpox which blisters

## SEVERITY ASSESSMENT

### Uncomplicated Measles
- Rash present, fever settling, child alert
- Feeding reduced but still drinking
- No fast breathing
- No diarrhoea or mild diarrhoea
- Eyes red but no corneal clouding or ulcers

### Complicated Measles — Urgent to Emergency
Any of the following:
- Fast breathing or chest indrawing — pneumonia
- Diarrhoea with dehydration
- Unable to eat or drink due to severe mouth ulcers
- Eyes — cloudiness, ulceration, or discharge beyond simple redness
- Seizure or altered consciousness — encephalitis
- Child with known malnutrition developing measles — always escalate
- Measles in a child under 1 year — higher complication risk
- Measles in a child known to be HIV-positive

---

## MANAGEMENT BY SEVERITY

### Uncomplicated Measles
- Urgent clinic visit — same day — for vitamin A supplementation
  and assessment
- Vitamin A immediately — two doses 24 hours apart — critical
  to prevent blindness and reduce mortality
- Paracetamol for fever
- Continue feeding and breastfeeding
- Plenty of fluids
- Eye care — clean eyes gently with clean cloth and water
- Keep child away from other unvaccinated children
- Do not use eye drops from patent medicine vendor without assessment

Plain language for caregiver:
"Your child has a serious infection that causes this rash and fever.
Your child needs to go to the clinic today for an important vitamin
that prevents the eyes from being damaged. Keep feeding and giving
fluids. Keep the child away from other children who may not be vaccinated."

### Complicated Measles
- Hospital urgently — same day or emergency depending on severity
- IV fluids if dehydrated
- Antibiotics for pneumonia or ear infection
- Eye assessment — corneal ulcer needs specialist care to prevent blindness
- Nutritional support — malnutrition worsens rapidly with measles

Plain language for caregiver:
"Your child's measles has caused a serious complication that needs
hospital treatment today. Please go now — complications from this
illness can be very dangerous if not treated quickly."

### Encephalitis or Severe Complication
- Emergency — hospital immediately
- This is life-threatening

## NIGERIAN SPECIFIC WARNINGS
- Vitamin A supplementation is the single most important intervention
  in any child with measles in Nigeria — must be given at first contact
- Two doses are needed — day 1 and day 2 — not just one dose
- Vitamin A deficiency is common in Kano due to poor dietary diversity —
  many children have low stores before measles even begins
- Measles vaccine is available free in Nigeria — given at 9 months and
  15 months — ask about vaccination status of all siblings
- In Kano, vaccine hesitancy exists in some communities — approach
  sensitively, explain that the vaccine prevents this serious illness
- Eye complications — corneal ulcer from vitamin A deficiency — can
  cause permanent blindness and are preventable with vitamin A
- Traditional eye treatments — putting substances into the eye — are
  common in northern Nigeria and worsen corneal damage significantly
- Malnourished children with measles — always escalate to hospital —
  mortality is dramatically higher in this group
- A child with measles is infectious from 4 days before to 4 days
  after the rash appears — household isolation advised
- All unvaccinated siblings should be vaccinated immediately when
  one child in the household develops measles
